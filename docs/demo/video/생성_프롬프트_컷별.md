# AI 영상 생성 프롬프트 (컷별)

파일명은 `output/video/clips/<컷 이름>.mp4` 로 저장하면 러프컷에 자동으로 들어갑니다. 각 컷은 **스토리보드 컷 이미지를 시작 프레임으로** 넣고 영상 프롬프트로 5~10초를 생성합니다. 생성기는 한 번에 5~10초라 컷 길이보다 길게 만들어 두면 러프컷이 앞부분을 씁니다.

## 공통 (모든 컷의 앞에 붙임)

**인물 고정 외형**
- Veteran: Korean man in his early sixties, short grey-white hair, deep smile lines, faded navy work jacket with worn reflective strips and a name patch, old scuffed white helmet, worn leather pocket notebook in his chest pocket.
- New Hire: Korean man in his early twenties, slim, short black hair, brand-new bright white helmet (from sheet 2 on: one small scuff), stiff clean navy work jacket, safety glasses pushed up, work gloves.
- Shift Lead: Korean woman in her thirties, hair tied back under a yellow helmet, navy work jacket with sleeves rolled once, clipboard.
- Next-Shift Worker: seen only from behind, orange helmet, navy work jacket, at night.

**스타일**: grounded semi-realistic cinematic look of a premium industrial brand film, cold-rolling steel coil line, steel grey with cool blue-green fluorescent light and warm amber machine lamps, soft haze, slow dolly or slider moves, no handheld shake, no speed ramps.
**금지**: no readable text, logos, subtitles or graphics anywhere in the frame (Korean text breaks; all text is added in editing). No dialogue.
**일관성**: the hydraulic power unit (HPU) must be the same machine in every shot: dark blue steel tank, round pressure gauge, tank sight glass, filter housing, cage frame.

초록 화면 컷(★)은 프롬프트 끝에 `camera locked off, completely static, the screen is a plain, evenly lit solid green rectangle with sharp edges and no reflections` 를 붙입니다.

## 시트 1 · 오프닝과 문제

### s1-01_GEN (6초) — 마지막 근무
Wide establishing shot at dawn, eye level, slow lateral slide right to left along the coil line. Huge silver coils on saddles, the HPU in the foreground, a wall clock above the control booth. By the booth a few workers gather around the Veteran with a small bouquet and a blank farewell banner; he bows slightly and smiles. SFX: low pump hum, distant coil clank, soft polite applause.

### s1-02_GEN (5초) — 펌프 소리를 듣는다
Medium shot, slightly low angle, very slow push-in. The Veteran stands alone beside the HPU, rests one bare hand flat on its painted housing, tilts his head and closes his eyes, listening. Amber indicator light on his grey hair and smile lines. SFX: pump rhythm rises, faint pressure tick, slow exhale. (엔딩 s5-06과 같은 구도로 쓰이므로 이 컷의 각도·거리를 기억해 둘 것)

### s1-03_GEN (4초) — 수첩이 떠난다
Close-up on the Veteran's weathered hands closing the worn leather notebook (pages dense with handwritten sketches and arrows) and sliding it into his chest pocket as he turns away. Behind him, softly out of focus, a locker with its nameplate removed. Static camera, focus racks from notebook to empty locker. (손·수첩이 일그러지면 실제 수첩 촬영으로 대체)

### s1-04_GEN (7초) — 문제 1: 처음 보는 상황
Medium shot, eye level, cooler colour temperature, weeks later. The New Hire stands alone before the same HPU. The tank sight glass shows milky foam, the pump sounds rougher, a small amber warning lamp blinks. He lifts his gloves halfway, stops, looks around for someone who is not there. Slow dolly-in that stops short, leaving space around him.

### s1-05_GEN (6초) — 답은 어딘가에
Close-up, three-quarter view of the New Hire's face, brand-new white helmet, safety glasses pushed up. His eyes move from the foaming sight glass to a thick English-language equipment manual binder on a nearby shelf and back. He swallows. Static camera with a slow rack focus from eyes to binder and back.

### s1-06_GEN (6초) — 문제 2: 말이 부족한 인수인계
Over-the-shoulder shot from behind the Shift Lead's yellow helmet. She reads a handwritten handover sheet on her clipboard with one short vague line; her pen hovers; she tilts the clipboard toward the light and frowns. The handwriting stays soft and unreadable. Gentle slide to the right over her shoulder.

### s1-07_CAM (5초) — 실물 촬영
폰으로 직접 촬영. 흰 종이에 급하게 쓴 손글씨 메모 "HPU 펌프 소음 → 확인 요망"과 밑줄 한 줄(수치·원인·다음 조치 없음). 매크로, 펜 끝이 멈춘다. AI로 만들면 글씨가 뭉개지므로 실제 종이로.

### s1-08_GENMG (6초) ★ — 문제 3: 밖으로 나갈 수 없는 공장망

### s1-09_GENMG (5초) — 그의 경험이 현장에 남아 있다면
Dynamic low-angle shot rising toward a small fanless edge computer and a rugged handheld PDA mounted beside the line, its screen dark. The haze thins slightly and the light warms a touch; the frame ends with clean empty space above the device. Smooth crane-up from floor level to eye level, ending centred on the device. 청록 선·로고는 `mg/s1-9_overlay.mp4`가 자동으로 얹힙니다.
